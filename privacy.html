Privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026. This covers the FitGymLog Android app.

What the app stores

Your workouts, routines, exercises and settings are stored on your phone. The developer runs no servers and receives none of your data. The app has no ads, analytics or accounts. If your phone's Android backup is on, Android includes the app's data in that backup to your own Google account, as it does for other apps; the coach's API key is left out.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you turn on sync, the app keeps one sync file in the hidden app data folder of your own Google Drive, using the drive.appdata permission. That permission lets the app see only its own folder, not your other Drive files. The file holds your workouts, routines and custom exercises so your devices stay in step. If you also turn on Keep coach notes in Google Drive, a second file there holds the coach's saved notes and workout notes. Only your Google account can reach these files.

Health Connect (optional)

If you turn it on, the app writes each finished workout to Health Connect on your phone: its type, start and end time, title, and a summary of sets, with each cardio block as a session of its own. Apps you allow, such as Samsung Health, can then read it there.

Coach (optional)

The coach is off unless you turn it on and enter an AI provider's address and your own API key. When you use it, the app sends that provider your recent workouts and sets, today's workout, your bodyweight, sex and goal, the daily calorie target the app works out, your messages to the coach and, if you allow it, the notes you saved. Height and birth year, if you enter them, are used only on the phone to work out that target. Nothing goes to the developer. The provider's own terms and privacy policy cover what it does with that data; some free tiers use requests to improve their products. The key is encrypted on your phone with the Android Keystore and is left out of backups. The chat, saved notes and workout notes stay on your phone, and in its Android backup, unless you also choose to keep the notes in Google Drive. Settings, Coach, Preview what is sent shows the exact request for today's targets.

Links and previews

To show exercise icons in lists and previews in a form guide, the app loads the linked page or video thumbnail directly from that site, such as YouTube, StrongLifts or StrengthLog. Those sites see an ordinary web request from your phone.

Files you import or export

Import and export use files you pick. The app reads or writes only those files.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your phone.
  • To delete the sync file, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, then Manage apps, and choose Delete hidden app data for FitGymLog.
  • To stop Drive access, remove FitGymLog at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Workouts written to Health Connect can be deleted in the Health Connect settings on your phone.

Contact

Questions go to GitHub issues.
