Privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026. This covers the FitGymLog Android app.

What the app stores

Your workouts, routines, exercises and settings are stored on your phone. The developer runs no servers and receives none of your data. The app has no ads, analytics or accounts.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you turn on sync, the app keeps one sync file in the hidden app data folder of your own Google Drive, using the drive.appdata permission. That permission lets the app see only its own folder, not your other Drive files. The file holds your workouts, routines and custom exercises so your devices stay in step. Only your Google account can reach it.

Health Connect (optional)

If you turn it on, the app writes each finished workout to Health Connect on your phone: its type, start and end time, title, and a summary of sets. Apps you allow, such as Samsung Health, can then read it there.

Links and previews

When you open a form guide, the app loads the linked page or video thumbnail directly from that site (for example YouTube or StrongLifts) to show a preview. Those sites see an ordinary web request from your phone.

Files you import or export

Import and export use files you pick. The app reads or writes only those files.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your phone.
  • To delete the sync file, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, then Manage apps, and choose Delete hidden app data for FitGymLog.
  • To stop Drive access, remove FitGymLog at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Workouts written to Health Connect can be deleted in the Health Connect settings on your phone.

Contact

Questions go to GitHub issues.
