Log the set from your wrist.

FitGymLog is a workout log for Android. Press play on your Galaxy Fit to complete a set, feel a buzz when rest is over, and load the bar by tapping plates.

Three buttons and a buzz

The band's music controls drive the workout, so the phone can stay in your bag.

  • Play or pauseCompletes the set and starts the rest timer.
  • NextMoves to the next exercise. After the last one it goes back to the first.
  • PreviousTakes one rep off the set on the band. Past zero it goes back to the planned reps, so a wrong press is easy to undo.
  • BuzzWhen rest is over, a notification buzzes the band and shows the next set.
One catch: music

The band's music buttons go to whichever app started playing most recently. With YouTube Music, Spotify or a form video playing, presses can switch back and forth between that app and FitGymLog. The rest buzz is a notification, so it always comes through.

Load the bar, not a keypad

Tap a plate to put one on each side, or tap a plate on the bar to take it off. Pick a 20, 15 or 10 kg bar (45, 35 or 25 lb), and one switch copies the weight to the sets after this one. Reps come from a number pad.

Form check between sets

Squat, bench press, deadlift, overhead press and barbell row come with StrongLifts' videos and written guides. Add a YouTube or web link to any exercise and it shows with its picture and title. Videos open in YouTube.

Unfold for the form guide

On an unfolded Galaxy Fold the current set, the rest timer and the form guide sit on the left, and the sets on the right keep their done boxes at the edge, under your thumb. History opens as a list with the workout beside it.

Set up in five sets

About five minutes. Tick them off as you go.

SetWhat to doDone
1Add a routine

On the Workout tab, StrongLifts 5x5 adds workouts A and B. Or build your own with New routine.
2Allow notifications

The end of each rest arrives as a notification. Settings, then Set up your Galaxy Fit, walks you through it.
3Turn on FitGymLog in Galaxy Wearable

In Galaxy Wearable, open Notifications and switch on FitGymLog. Only apps switched on there reach the band.
4Send a test buzz

Tap the test button, then lock your phone. The band should buzz about five seconds later.
5Optional: Samsung Health and Drive

In Settings, turn on Write workouts to Health Connect, and Connect Google Drive to keep two phones in step.

Your data stays with you

On your phone
Workouts live on your phone, and backups and exports are files you keep. No account, no ads, no analytics.
Samsung Health
FitGymLog writes every set to Health Connect. Samsung Health picks the workout up from there, but it doesn't show the individual sets.
Google Drive sync, in your Drive
Optional. One file in a hidden folder of your own Drive keeps your phones in step. The app cannot see your other files.
